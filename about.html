Our Story

Crafting Spaces,
Building Memories

Furniro was born from a single, enduring belief — that every home deserves furniture as unique as the people who live in it. Founded in 2012 by a team of passionate designers and seasoned craftsmen, we set out to bridge the gap between high-end artistry and everyday affordability.

From our workshops in Coral Gables, Florida, each piece is conceived with intention: thoughtfully sourced materials, meticulous joinery, and a design language that respects both modern minimalism and timeless warmth. We don't just make furniture — we create the backdrop for your most important moments.

Today, Furniro serves thousands of families and design professionals across 15 countries, staying true to the craft principles that started it all.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every decision we make — from material sourcing to final delivery — is guided by four principles we refuse to compromise on.

Craftsmanship Excellence

We partner with skilled artisans who have dedicated their lives to their craft. Every joint, finish, and curve is executed with unwavering precision.

Sustainable Sourcing

We responsibly source materials from certified forests and eco-conscious suppliers, minimising our environmental footprint without sacrificing quality.

Modern Innovation

Tradition informs our craft but never limits it. We continuously explore new design languages, smart materials, and ergonomic research to stay ahead.

Customer Devotion

Our relationship doesn't end at checkout. We stand by every piece with dedicated after-sales support, transparent policies, and genuine care.

Our Difference

Why Thousands Choose Furniro

Good furniture should outlast trends. Here is how we make sure ours does.

Premium Materials Only

Every piece of wood is hand-selected. Every fabric is tested for durability and feel. We use only top-tier materials that age beautifully and stand the test of daily life.

Handcrafted with Time

We don't rush production. Each Furniro piece receives up to 60 hours of skilled handwork, ensuring that what arrives in your home is finished with care — not on a conveyor belt.

Free White-Glove Delivery

Orders above $150 are delivered free, right to the room of your choice. Our delivery teams are trained to handle every piece with the respect it deserves from workshop to doorstep.

2-Year Warranty & Easy Returns

Every Furniro product is protected by a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering structural defects. Changed your mind? Our 30-day hassle-free return policy has you covered.

In-House Design Expertise

Our team of 12 in-house designers works closely with each production run, refining proportions and details that catalogues never show you. The result: furniture that looks as considered as it feels.

Dedicated 24/7 Support

Whether you need help choosing the right piece, tracking your delivery, or sorting a warranty claim — our support team is available around the clock, seven days a week.
